This project involves a platform that acts as a vital signs monitor, allowing users to quickly and intuitively view general health statistics. While data can be entered directly into the platform, I am also developing a hardware component a sensor similar to a smartwatch that works in conjunction with the app; data would be transmitted from the sensor to the app via a USB cable, though the device is not essential for the app's core functionality. AI will be integrated into the application to provide users with immediate answers to questions about their health metrics and offer advice on improving their health. Start date: September 26, 2022.
